# Elements Documentation



## 0. Air

### Main Quirk
Empty space. Flashes white when contaminated by Uranium radiation.


## 1. Sand

### Main Quirk
Classic falling powder. Falls straight down, or diagonally down-left / down-right. Sinks through **Water** and **Oil** by swapping downward or diagonally downward. Supports a secret rainbow gradient mode (unlocks `"Eye Candy"`).


## 2. Water

### Main Quirk
Standard liquid. Falls straight down into **Air** or **Oil**, or spreads left/right (randomized order) into **Air** or **Oil**. Can evaporate into steam under the right conditions. Can unlock `"Chemistry!"`.


## 3. Steel

### Main Quirk
Passive, immovable solid. Resists **fire**, gravity, and crushing. Shiny when next to **fire**.


## 4. Acid

### Main Quirk
Corrosive liquid. Reacts violently with water, wood, and other organic materials. Can unlock `"Chemistry!"`.


## 5. Steam

### Main Quirk
Condensing gas. Rises straight up or diagonally up or spreads left/right. If the cell directly above it has type __solid__, __glass__, or __pump__, or if steam is on the top, it has a 1% chance per update to condense into **Water**.


## 6. Dry Wood

### Main Quirk
Vertically falling, absorbent flammable block. Unlike Sand, it does **not** slide diagonally—it only falls straight down. Can unlock `"Spontaneous Combustion!"`.


## 7. Wet Wood

### Main Quirk
Shows absolutely no effect from radiation, immovable solid created when **Dry Wood**, **Gunpowder**, or **Fuse** absorbs **Water**.


## 8. Fire

### Main Quirk
Illuminating flame. It has a 20% chance per update to burn out into **Air** if it can't feed on flammable things, otherwise feeds and spreads through that material. Can unlock `"Spontaneous Combustion!"`.


## 9. Lava

### Main Quirk
Slow, Illuminating, superheated destructive liquid. Can ignite the top surface with **fire** sometimes, and flows slowly. 
Can unlock `"Flint... and Steel..."`.


## 10. Obsidian

### Main Quirk
Radiation-shielding solid that can collapse under excessive vertical weight. Blocks **Uranium** radiation rays completely.


## 11. Gunpowder

### Main Quirk
Highly flammable falling powder. Once ignited on **fire**, it burns very fast.


## 12. Fuse

### Main Quirk
Stationary flammable solid. Stays fixed in place regardless of gravity and burns just as fast as **gunpowder**.


## 13. Uranium

### Main Quirk
Radioactive falling powder. Has a small chance to emit a radiation ray in a random direction. If that ray successfully reacts with something, gets more corroded and can die after a while.


## 14. Crystal

### Main Quirk
Luminescent, light-amplifying, and slow-growing solid (when under water and in dark). Tints downstream cells with an oscillating hue. Its own color oscillates between hues as well. Can be hyper-activated by **acid** or **uranium**.


## 15. Glass

### Main Quirk
Fully transparent solid that focuses **Uranium** radiation toward the player's pointer. Can unlock `"Transparent"`.


## 16. Graphite

### Main Quirk
Light blocking solid, reflects radiation rays like a mirror, and is slippery for powders.


## 17. Ice

### Main Quirk
Freezing solid. Can also cool nearby **uranium** to extend its life.


## 18. Oil

### Main Quirk
Light, flammable liquid. Less dense than **Water** and **Sand**, which both sink through it. Can unlock `"Spontaneous Combustion!"`.


## 19. Fumes

### Main Quirk
Non-condensing, flammable gas. Burns rapidly when exposed to **fire**. Never condenses back into liquid.


## 20. Morph

### Main Quirk
Shape-shifting element. Becomes whatever element it touches, and the chain reaction continues. Can unlock `"Copy Cat!"`.


## 21. Pump

### Main Quirk
Gravity-aligned element duplicator. **Pump** copies the above cell's state into the cell below without consuming the source cell.


## 22. Void

### Main Quirk
Immovable element acting like a black hole. Eats anything and everything it touches. Can unlock `"His Gluttony Sickens Me."`.
