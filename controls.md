# **Controls** for _The Fluid Sim_

## Movement and Action

- `WASD` to **move** around.
- `SPACE` to **paint**.

## Brush Control

- `Q` & `E` cycle through **brush types** (elements).
- `Shift + Q` and `Shift + E` to cycle through **brush shapes**.
- `=` & `-`to cycle through **brush sizes**.

## Shifting Gravity

- `→↑↓←` for shifting **gravity**.

## Time Manipulation

- `\` to **pause** or **unpause**.
- `[` & `]` to cycle through **framerate**, if the simulation is _unpaused_.
- `[` & `]` to **step** back or forwards in the simulation, if _paused_.

## Miscellaneous 

- `Shift + C` to **clear** the entire sandbox.
- `` ` `` to stop **cursor RGB**.
